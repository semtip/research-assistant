SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW. All people, contact details and the service "Loop" are fictional.
Study: Loop e-bike subscription, user experience research
Participant: Sanne de Vries, 34, daily commuter, subscriber for 8 months
Interviewer: Mila

Mila: Thanks for joining, Sanne. Can you start by telling me how you use your Loop bike?

Sanne de Vries: Sure. I ride it to work every day, about twenty minutes each way. Before Loop I took the bus, so honestly the bike itself changed my mornings. It's comfortable, it looks great, and I arrive without being sweaty. I really love the bike.

Mila: What was it like to get started?

Sanne de Vries: The delivery was lovely, the guy who brought it explained everything and adjusted the saddle. Signing up in the app was less fun. The ID check kept rejecting my passport photo, I tried it maybe six times. In the end I just gave up and did it the next day on my laptop.

Mila: What happens when something goes wrong with the bike?

Sanne de Vries: That's the big one for me. In October my rear brake started making a grinding noise. I reported it in the app and they said a mechanic would come within 48 hours. It took nine days. Nine days without my bike, and I still paid for that month. I had to take the bus again and I was late twice.

Mila: How did you try to get help during those nine days?

Sanne de Vries: I tried to call, but there's no phone number anywhere. You can only talk to the chat assistant, and it kept sending me the same article about booking a repair. I just wanted a person to tell me when someone would come. I even emailed sanne.devries@mailbox.nl to myself as a reminder to complain, that's how annoyed I was.

Mila: Is there anything about the costs you'd like to share?

Sanne de Vries: The monthly price is fine, I knew it when I signed up. But when they finally fixed the brake, I got an extra charge of 35 euros for "wear outside normal use". Nobody told me that could happen. I couldn't find it anywhere in the app. That felt really unfair because I just ride to work.

Mila: How has the battery been?

Sanne de Vries: No complaints. I charge it twice a week and that's plenty for my commute.

Mila: Anything else you'd change?

Sanne de Vries: Just fix the repairs. If a mechanic came within two days like they promise, I'd recommend Loop to everyone. Right now I tell friends "great bike, but pray it doesn't break".
