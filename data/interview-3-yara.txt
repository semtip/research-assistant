SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW. All people, contact details and the service "Loop" are fictional.
Study: Loop e-bike subscription, user experience research
Participant: Yara El Amrani, 26, master's student, subscriber for 4 months
Interviewer: Mila

Mila: Hi Yara, welcome. Tell me a bit about how you use Loop.

Yara El Amrani: I use it for everything. Campus, my side job at a cafe, going out. I don't have a car, so the bike is basically my car.

Mila: What did you think of the sign-up?

Yara El Amrani: It was fine for me, it took like five minutes. The app is pretty clean. I think it's designed for people like me, honestly.

Mila: Have you had any issues with the bike?

Yara El Amrani: Yes, the chain kept falling off. I booked a repair and the slot they gave me was a week later. A week! I had exams that week. I ended up borrowing my roommate's old bike, which was terrible. When you depend on the bike every day, waiting a week is just not an option.

Mila: Were there any costs involved?

Yara El Amrani: That's actually what annoyed me most. I returned the bike two days late when I switched to a different plan, because the pickup slot didn't work for me. They charged me a late fee of 20 euros per day. I had no idea that existed. It's somewhere in the terms, I guess, but who reads that? It should be in the app, right where you book the pickup.

Mila: How do you usually contact Loop?

Yara El Amrani: Through the chat, and to be fair it's fast. For simple things it's fine.

Mila: Is there anything you would add to the service?

Yara El Amrani: I'd love it if I could see how much battery I have left on my phone. Now I have to walk to the bike to check. Small thing, but it would help. You can send me the summary at yara.elamrani@studentmail.nl if you want.

Mila: Last question. Would you recommend Loop?

Yara El Amrani: Yes, but with a warning about the fees and the repairs. The bike itself is really good.
