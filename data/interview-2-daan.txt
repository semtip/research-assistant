SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW. All people, contact details and the service "Loop" are fictional.
Study: Loop e-bike subscription, user experience research
Participant: Daan Bakker, 52, uses the bike for groceries and visiting family, subscriber for 1 year
Interviewer: Mila

Mila: Hi Daan, thanks for making time. How do you use your Loop bike?

Daan Bakker: Mostly for groceries and to visit my mother on the other side of town. A few times a week. I'm not a sporty person, so the electric support is great on the bridge.

Mila: How was signing up?

Daan Bakker: My daughter helped me, otherwise I would not have managed. The app asked me to scan my ID and then it said "verification failed" without saying why. We tried with my driving licence instead and that worked, but it took us the whole evening.

Mila: Has the bike needed any repairs?

Daan Bakker: Twice. The first time was a flat tyre and that was fixed quickly, the next day. The second time the motor stopped working completely. They said it would be two days, but it was more than a week before the replacement bike came. I had to carry my groceries by hand. At my age that's no joke.

Mila: How did you feel about how Loop handled that?

Daan Bakker: I wanted to call someone, but you can't. There's only the chat. I typed my question three times and every time it answered something else. In the end my daughter found an email address and wrote to them. If you want to reach me about this research, my number is 06 1234 5678, by the way, I prefer the phone.

Mila: What do you think about the price?

Daan Bakker: The price is fair for what you get. I compared it with buying an e-bike and this is easier, no worries about theft insurance.

Mila: How does the battery hold up?

Daan Bakker: In summer, no problem at all. But since it got colder I notice it runs out much faster. Last week it died halfway home from my mother's place. Now I only go if I'm sure it's fully charged. I don't really trust it anymore in winter.

Mila: Is there anything you'd like to be different?

Daan Bakker: In the summer I go to my sister in Spain for six weeks. I asked if I could pause my subscription and they said no, I can only cancel. So I pay six weeks for a bike that stands in my shed. That doesn't feel right.

Mila: Anything else?

Daan Bakker: I've never had any trouble with the lock, which my neighbour warned me about. That part works well.
